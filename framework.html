The 5-Point Inspection Framework

Every website we analyze is subjected to our proprietary inspection system. We don't just look at keywords; we look at the entire engine that powers search visibility and user experience.

1

The SERP Appearance

"The Click Test"

Does the website earn the click before the user even arrives? Your search result is your first impression, and it needs to stand out among competitors. We analyze every element that appears in Google's search results.

Title Tag Hygiene

We check if titles are truncated, keyword-missing, or uncompelling. Your title is your headline in the SERP.

Meta Description

We analyze if the description acts as a valid sales pitch or just generic filler that fails to compel clicks.

Visual Trust

We look for custom favicons and rich snippets (stars, prices, images) that make the result stand out in Google.

2

Technical Health

"The Robot Test"

Can search engines crawl and index the site without errors? Technical SEO is the foundation. If Google can't properly access and understand your site, nothing else matters.

Core Web Vitals

We measure load speed (LCP), interactivity (FID/INP), and visual stability (CLS) against Google's thresholds.

Mobile Usability

We verify that the site functions flawlessly on phones, checking for text size and touch-target spacing.

Clean Code

We scan for broken links (404s), messy URL structures, and improper redirects that waste "crawl budget."

3

On-Page Structure

"The Skim Test"

Is the content formatted for both humans and bots? Users skim, and so does Google. Your page structure needs to make sense at a glance while providing semantic clarity for search engines.

Header Hierarchy

We audit the H1, H2, and H3 tags to ensure a logical outline that Google can understand and users can navigate.

Media Optimization

We check if images have descriptive Alt Text and proper file names for both accessibility and SEO.

Natural Language

We analyze the text for "keyword stuffing" vs. natural, helpful phrasing that serves user intent.

4

Content & Authority

"The Trust Test"

Does the site demonstrate the expertise required to rank? Google's E-E-A-T guidelines emphasize trust signals. We evaluate whether your content proves you're worth ranking.

E-E-A-T Compliance

We look for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals (author bios, About pages, addresses).

Depth & Value

We evaluate if the content actually answers the user's intent or just fills space with thin content.

Internal Linking

We check how well the site connects its own pages to keep users engaged and distribute authority.

5

Conversion Optimization

"The Money Test"

Does the traffic actually lead to business results? Ranking is meaningless if visitors don't convert. We analyze the user journey from landing to action.

Call to Action (CTA)

We critique the visibility and clarity of the "Buy," "Subscribe," or "Contact" buttons throughout the site.

User Path

We assess how easy it is for a visitor to understand what the company offers within 5 seconds of landing.

Distraction Free

We identify annoying pop-ups or clutter that drive users away before they can convert.

Our Grading System

Each teardown concludes with a Final Grade (A-F) and a clear "Fix-It" roadmap.

A

Excellent

Exceptional performance across all five inspection points. Minor optimizations may be possible, but the foundation is rock solid.

B

Good

Strong performance with room for improvement. The site follows best practices but has specific areas that need attention.

C

Average

Meets basic standards but has significant gaps. Multiple inspection points need work to compete effectively in search.

D

Below Average

Serious issues across multiple areas. The site is likely struggling to rank and needs substantial improvements.

F

Failing

Critical problems that severely impact search visibility and user experience. Immediate action required on fundamentals.

Fix-It Roadmap

Every grade comes with a prioritized action plan, turning analysis into practical steps you can implement immediately.
