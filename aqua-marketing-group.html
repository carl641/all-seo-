The SERP Appearance

"The Click Test"

A-
Passed

Title Tag Hygiene

Their title tag is "Aqua Marketing Group | Digital Marketing Services in Alabama". This is textbook execution:

Brand Name + Core Keyword + Location

It tells Google exactly what they do and where they do it. This structure maximizes click-through rate while maintaining keyword relevance for local searches.

Passed

Visual Trust

The site uses consistent branding (water/waves theme) that likely translates to their favicon. The URL structure suggests they are targeting specific local queries (e.g., mobile-marketing-agency.html), which likely generates rich local snippets in search results.

Technical Health

"The Robot Test"

B+
Excellent

URL Structure

Instead of generic URLs like /locations/mobile, they use keyword-rich URLs:

mobile-marketing-agency.html
daphne-marketing-agency.html
gulf-shores-marketing-agency.html

This is a massive SEO win. It reinforces the keyword "Marketing Agency" for every single location they service. Each URL is optimized for the exact search query a potential customer would use.

Mobile Usability

The link structure is clean, and the content is chunked into logical sections (Services, Locations, Testimonials) that stack well on mobile devices. No horizontal scrolling issues detected.

Passed

Code Bloat

The site uses lightweight SVG images (images/about-wave.svg) for graphics, which keeps load times fast compared to heavy PNGs. This is a smart technical choice that improves Core Web Vitals.

3

On-Page Structure

"The Skim Test"

A
Mixed

Header Hierarchy

The Good: They use H2s and H3s perfectly for services and locations. The content structure flows logically and helps both users and search engines understand the page organization.

The Critique: Their H1 is "Make Waves in the Digital World." While creative and on-brand, it is weak for SEO. Google gives the most weight to the H1 tag. "Make Waves" is not a keyword anyone searches for.

The Fix

Change the H1 to "Digital Marketing Agency in Alabama" and move "Make Waves in the Digital World" to the sub-headline (H2 or tagline). This preserves the creative branding while capturing the primary keyword.

Current: <h1>Make Waves in the Digital World</h1>

Recommended: <h1>Digital Marketing Agency in Alabama</h1>
<p class="tagline">Make Waves in the Digital World</p>
Passed

Image Alt Text

They are actually using Alt Text correctly:

<img src="images/mobile.jpg" alt="Mobile Alabama Digital Marketing">

Most agencies forget this; Aqua MG nailed it. This helps with image search visibility and accessibility compliance.

Content & Authority

"The Trust Test"

A
Excellent

Local Authority

They don't just say "We serve Alabama." They have built dedicated pages for Mobile, Daphne, Gulf Shores, and Orange Beach.

This is the gold standard for Local SEO. It allows them to rank for "SEO in Daphne" and "PPC in Mobile" simultaneously. Each location page becomes a targeted landing page for local search queries.

Passed

E-E-A-T Signals

They list a real physical address (3223 Springhill Ave) and a local phone number (251 area code). This proves to Google they are a real business, not a generic dropshipping site.

These "NAP" (Name, Address, Phone) signals are critical for local search rankings and help build trust with both search engines and potential customers.

Passed

Social Proof

The testimonials use Full Names + Business Titles:

"Darren Thibodaux, Owner, Gulf Coast Realty"

This adds verifiable trust that anonymous reviews ("John D.") lack. Named testimonials with business affiliations significantly increase credibility.

5

Conversion Optimization

"The Money Test"

B
Good

Call to Action (CTA)

They have clear "Get Started" and "Get Your Free Consultation" buttons. The CTAs are visible and use action-oriented language that tells visitors exactly what will happen next.

Good

Clarity

The services are explained simply: "We optimize your online presence..." The language is accessible and avoids industry jargon that might confuse potential clients.

Opportunity

Missed Opportunity

The hero section (top of the page) is a bit text-heavy with the brand message. A more direct value proposition might convert cold traffic faster than the "Make Waves" pun.

Consider testing: "We grow Alabama businesses by 200%" or "The #1 Digital Marketing Agency in Mobile, AL" - something that immediately communicates concrete value.

The One Thing They Must Fix

The H1 Tag. They are sacrificing SEO power for creativity. By changing the H1 from "Make Waves in the Digital World" to "Premier Digital Marketing Agency in Alabama", they would likely see an immediate lift in rankings for their main head term.

The brand message can still be prominent - just not in the most SEO-critical element on the page.

Key Takeaways from This Teardown

Location Pages Win Local SEO

Creating dedicated pages for each service area (Mobile, Daphne, Gulf Shores) is the gold standard for ranking in multiple local markets.

Keyword-Rich URLs Matter

Using mobile-marketing-agency.html instead of /locations/mobile reinforces keywords in every URL.

H1 Tags Are for Keywords

Save clever taglines for sub-headlines. Your H1 should contain the primary keyword you want to rank for.

Named Testimonials Build Trust

Full names with business titles ("Darren Thibodaux, Owner, Gulf Coast Realty") are far more credible than anonymous reviews.
