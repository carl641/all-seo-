The 5-Point Inspection Framework

We don't just look at keywords; we look at the entire engine.

Point 1

The SERP Appearance

"The Click Test"

Does the website earn the click before the user even arrives? We analyze title tags, meta descriptions, and visual trust signals.

  • Title Tag Hygiene
  • Meta Description Quality
  • Visual Trust (Favicons, Rich Snippets)
Point 2

Technical Health

"The Robot Test"

Can search engines crawl and index the site without errors? We measure performance metrics and identify technical issues.

  • Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS)
  • Mobile Usability
  • Clean Code & URL Structure
Point 3

On-Page Structure

"The Skim Test"

Is the content formatted for both humans and bots? We audit the content hierarchy and optimization.

  • Header Hierarchy (H1-H3)
  • Media Optimization & Alt Text
  • Natural Language vs. Keyword Stuffing
Point 4

Content & Authority

"The Trust Test"

Does the site demonstrate the expertise required to rank? We evaluate E-E-A-T signals and content depth.

  • E-E-A-T Compliance
  • Content Depth & Value
  • Internal Linking Strategy
