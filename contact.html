Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our audit process.

How long does an audit take?

Most audits are completed within 5-7 business days. Complex sites with hundreds of pages may take longer. We'll let you know the timeline when we begin.

What do I receive?

You'll receive a comprehensive report covering all 5 points of our framework, an overall A-F grade, individual scores for each category, and a prioritized Fix-It roadmap.

Will my audit be published?

Only with your permission. If you'd like your site featured as a public teardown, we'll discuss this with you. Otherwise, your audit remains completely private.

Can you help implement fixes?

Our primary service is the audit itself. However, we can discuss implementation support or recommend trusted partners if you need hands-on help.

What if I disagree with the grade?

We're happy to explain our reasoning for any score. Our methodology is transparent, and we'll walk you through exactly how we evaluated each aspect of your site.

Do you offer ongoing monitoring?

We can discuss follow-up audits to track your progress. Many clients return after implementing our recommendations to see their improved grade.
